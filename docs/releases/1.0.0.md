Forzac 1.0.0: measured, and promised to stay this way.

Since 0.9.0 Forzac has grown a Books folder and a library like Calibre's, two pages side by side, updates that install
themselves on the Mac, books sent to a Kindle, reading shared with the Android app and your other computers, plain
text and Markdown, and the month's reading in stats. 1.0 adds no new feature. It checks that Forzac is quick to open,
smooth to turn and quiet while you read, against fixed budgets on real machines, and fixes what the checks found.

- **Books open faster on the Mac.** A book window used to lay out its chapter twice, once at its default size and once
  at the size that fits the screen or that you left it at. Now it does it once, and the keys card is made only when
  you first press `?`. A book that keeps its whole text in one chapter shows its first page in 0.4 s instead of 1.1 s.
- On the Mac the update line in a narrow library ends in "…" instead of losing its link.
- On Linux `Esc` closes the keys card in the library instead of the window, and the update link no longer shows a
  focus ring.

Check a download with `shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`.

## The budgets, and how they were measured

Measured with `scripts/measure.sh` (Mac) and `scripts/pi-headless.sh` (Raspberry Pi 5); the budgets are in
`openspec/specs/performance`. Books: an FB2 novel (*Колыбель для кошки*, 300 pages) and EPUBs with chapters.

| | Budget | MacBook Pro M1, macOS 14.5 | Raspberry Pi 5, Raspberry Pi OS 13 |
|---|---|---|---|
| First page, warm | 150 ms on the Mac from opening the book, 200 ms on the Pi from the start | 128–144 ms (190–205 ms from launch), screen locked: to repeat unlocked | not yet measured |
| Missed frames in 20 page turns | 0, at 60 Hz on the Mac, at 120 Hz on the Pi | 0 of 239 | not yet measured |
| CPU while idle, 10 s | 0 ticks | 0 | not yet measured |
| Slowest chapter's layout | 50 ms on the Pi | 3 ms (the FB2), 18 and 31 ms (EPUB chapters of 31 and 48 pages) | not yet measured |

## What was checked, and what was not

- Mac: the measurements above on 2026-10-23, the display awake but the screen locked; the screens in Light and Dark,
  English and Russian, on an M1 MacBook Pro with macOS 14.
- Linux: the AUR packages built and installed with `makepkg -si` in an x86_64 Arch container, and run under a headless
  sway; the library's keys, search, theme and first frame checked under Xvfb.
- Not measured on hardware: x86_64 Linux and Hyprland. If you run Omarchy, please tell us in the issues how Forzac
  opens, turns pages and tiles there.

## Known issues

- A book whose whole text is one chapter (some FB2s converted to EPUB) shows its first page only after that chapter
  is laid out: 0.4 s on the M1 for 316 pages.
