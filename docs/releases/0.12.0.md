Forzac 0.12.0: a library like Calibre's, and books sent to a Kindle.

## What is new

- **Views.** Tabs under the library's head: Recent (as before), Title, Author, Series and Shelves (`Tab` switches; the
  view is remembered). Author and Series group the books under headings, a series in its order. Books now carry their
  series and number, genres and year from FB2, FB3 and EPUB; a book's series shows under its cover, and search finds
  series too.
- **Shelves.** "Add to shelf" in a book's "⋯" menu (or a right click) puts it on your own shelves, with "New shelf…";
  the Shelves tab shows Reading, Not started and Finished first, then yours.
- **About the book and editing it.** "About the book" shows the cover, series, genres, year, language and the
  annotation (`i`). "Edit…" fixes the title, authors, series and genres; Forzac keeps the edits, the file is never
  changed, and "From the book" puts its own details back.
- **Send to Kindle.** "Send to Kindle" in a book's menu converts it to MOBI — what Calibre sends to a Kindle — and
  copies it onto a Kindle connected by cable, with its cover in the Kindle's list, the contents in "Go to", notes and
  images. Kindles from before 2024 (they show as a disk); macOS asks once for access to the removable volume.
- **Folders.** Drop a folder on the library, press `Ctrl+Shift+O` (`⇧⌘O`) or choose "Add a Folder…" to add every book
  in it and its subfolders. "Change the Books folder…" moves your books to another folder — one kept by Dropbox,
  iCloud Drive or Syncthing, say — or uses it as it is. Both are in the new "⋯" menu in the library's head.
- **macOS: the reading bar.** The page fills the window under a transparent title bar; a bar with Contents, text
  settings, search and the bookmark comes with the pointer and fades away. The menus' ⌘ shortcuts work in the Russian
  layout.
- **Two-page spread:** the book's title over the left page, the chapter's over the right, as in a printed book.
- **Book ids as on Android.** A book is now known by the SHA-256 of its file, as in the Android app — the base for
  syncing the two later. The first start moves your positions, bookmarks and highlights to the new ids once; the first
  library after the update reads every book's file again for its series (a few seconds for a large library).

## Downloads

| | |
|---|---|
| Mac, Apple Silicon, macOS 14+ | `Forzac-macos-arm64.dmg` — signed ad hoc, not notarised: on the first open macOS blocks it; allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway ([step by step](https://github.com/denisfl/forzac-desktop/blob/main/docs/macos-first-open.md)). 0.11.0 offers this version in the library's line and installs it itself |
| Arch, Omarchy | The AUR packages are waiting for AUR registration to reopen. Meanwhile the same PKGBUILD from this repository: `git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/denisfl/forzac-desktop && cd forzac-desktop/packaging/aur/forzac-bin && makepkg -si` |
| Debian 13, Raspberry Pi OS 13 (arm64) | `forzac-linux-arm64.deb`: `sudo apt install ./forzac-linux-arm64.deb` |
| Other Linux with GTK 4.14+ | `forzac-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, `forzac-linux-aarch64.tar.gz`: unpack and run `forzac-0.12.0-linux-<arch>/bin/forzac` |

Check a download with `shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`.

## What was checked, and what was not

- macOS, by hand on an M1 MacBook Pro with macOS 14: the move to the new book ids on a copy of a real 0.11.0 state;
  adding a folder; the views, shelves, About the book and editing in English and Russian; changing the Books folder
  (Move and Leave them); Send to Kindle onto a Kindle with firmware 5.19 (the book listed with its cover, it opens, "Go
  to" lists its chapters).
- Linux: built, linted and tested; the library's tabs and Shelves view seen as screenshots under a headless Weston.
  The new menus, sheets and dialogs of the GTK library were not tried by hand on a Linux desktop, and nothing was
  measured on a Raspberry Pi 5 for this release.
