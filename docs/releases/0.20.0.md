Forzac 0.20.0: PDF, formulas, picture books and Standard Ebooks.

The desktop reader now does what Forzac for Android 1.0.4 does, on the Mac and on Linux.

- **PDF, as on paper.** A PDF opens as a book and goes on the shelf with its first page as the cover and its title and
  author. Pages keep their colours on white in every theme; facing pages show as in a printed book in a wide window.
  Zoom to 4× with `+` `−` `0`, a pinch, `Ctrl`+wheel or a double click; contents from the outline, search with the word
  marked on its page, links with a thin line under them and the "← Back" pill. On Linux PDFium draws the pages and comes
  with the packages.
- **Formulas.** MathML in EPUB is typeset: scripts, fractions, sums with limits, roots, matrices, stretched brackets.
  A formula wider than the column shrinks and opens whole on a click. STIX Two Math comes along for the signs a book's
  font lacks.
- **Picture books.** A fixed-layout EPUB of pages (manga, comics, children's books) opens page by page, each page whole,
  right to left when the book is.
- **Standard Ebooks** read as they mean it: small capitals, SVG images, line art in the theme's ink, and a new book opens
  where its text begins rather than at the imprint.
- **The shelf** sorts as on the phone: without leading quotes and "The", "A", "An", your alphabet first. Under the
  covers a row's shares stand on one line.
- **Justified text** has fewer holes: a short word like "в" or "и" may end a line where keeping it would leave wide gaps.
- **The reading menu** shows the time left in the book under the chapter's.
- On Linux Forzac is offered for PDF, plain text, a zip and XML in "Open with"; the screen stays on while you read a PDF
  or a picture book on the Mac.

## Downloads

| | |
|---|---|
| Mac, Apple Silicon, macOS 14+ | `Forzac-macos-arm64.dmg`. If you have 0.11 or later, the library offers the update itself. A first download is signed ad hoc, not notarised: allow it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway ([step by step](https://github.com/denisfl/forzac-desktop/blob/main/docs/macos-first-open.md)) |
| Arch, Omarchy | The AUR packages are waiting for AUR registration to reopen. Meanwhile the same PKGBUILD from this repository: `git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/denisfl/forzac-desktop && cd forzac-desktop/packaging/aur/forzac-bin && makepkg -si` |
| Debian 13, Raspberry Pi OS 13 (arm64) | `forzac-linux-arm64.deb`: `sudo apt install ./forzac-linux-arm64.deb` |
| Other Linux with GTK 4.14+ | `forzac-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, `forzac-linux-aarch64.tar.gz`: unpack and run `forzac-0.20.0-linux-<arch>/bin/forzac` |

Check a download with `shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`.

## What was checked, and what was not

- Core tests, both shells built and linted; on the M1 MacBook Pro the PDF, picture-book and formula windows, turning at
  120 Hz (no more late frames than a novel), the library; the Linux shell under a headless Weston and the Arch packages
  in a container.
- Books without formulas are typeset exactly as in 0.19 (30 books compared page by page); Russian books' lines change
  where a short word now ends a line.
- Not tried yet: the Raspberry Pi 5 (waived for this release), Omarchy on hardware, the Linux reading panel's keys on a
  real desktop, a publisher's textbook with MathML (a hand-written one was used).
- Known: once, in a container, the Linux build stopped with an error as a window closed; it did not happen again in 38
  runs.
