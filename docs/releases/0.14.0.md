Forzac 0.14.0: your reading shared with the Android app and your other computers, and a settings window.

## What is new

- **Sync through your Books folder.** Put the Books folder in Dropbox, iCloud Drive or Syncthing ("Change the Books
  folder…" in the library's "⋯" menu), and Forzac shares where you are in each book, your bookmarks and highlights,
  your shelves and your edits of book details with Forzac on your other computers and on Android (0.9.7 and later:
  the arrows button in its library → "Choose folder"; on Android the folder has to be a local one, which Syncthing
  keeps). There is nothing to set up on the computer: each device writes a small file of its own into `.forzac/` in
  the Books folder, so the sync service never sees two devices writing one file and makes no conflicted copies.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Forzac gives your reading away when you leave it (switch to another app, close a
  book, quit) and takes the other devices' when you come back. A book you have open moves to the place you read up to
  elsewhere, and "back" (`⌃O`) returns to where it was.
- **Settings.** "Settings…" in the library's "⋯" menu (`⌘,` on the Mac outside a book): the interface language (as
  the system, English or Русский, applied at the next start) and syncing explained — how it works, the Books folder
  with "Change…", when it last synced or what went wrong, the other devices in the folder, and "Sync Now".
- **The latest change wins**, item by item: a bookmark removed on the phone is removed on the Mac, a highlight made on
  the Mac appears on the phone. Places are found again by their text when the two apps number a book's paragraphs
  differently. Reading data for a book that is not in the folder yet waits for it.

## Downloads

| | |
|---|---|
| Mac, Apple Silicon, macOS 14+ | `Forzac-macos-arm64.dmg` — signed ad hoc, not notarised: on the first open macOS blocks it; allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway ([step by step](https://github.com/denisfl/forzac-desktop/blob/main/docs/macos-first-open.md)). 0.11.0 and later offer this version in the library's line and install it themselves |
| Arch, Omarchy | The AUR packages are waiting for AUR registration to reopen. Meanwhile the same PKGBUILD from this repository: `git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/denisfl/forzac-desktop && cd forzac-desktop/packaging/aur/forzac-bin && makepkg -si` |
| Debian 13, Raspberry Pi OS 13 (arm64) | `forzac-linux-arm64.deb`: `sudo apt install ./forzac-linux-arm64.deb` |
| Other Linux with GTK 4.14+ | `forzac-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, `forzac-linux-aarch64.tar.gz`: unpack and run `forzac-0.14.0-linux-<arch>/bin/forzac` |

Check a download with `shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`.

## What was checked, and what was not

- macOS, by hand on an M1 MacBook Pro with macOS 14, with a Nothing Phone (2) running Forzac for Android (the shared
  folder copied over USB in place of a sync service): a place, bookmarks and a highlight made on the Mac open on the
  phone; a place read on the phone moves the book open on the Mac when it comes back to the front, with "back".
- The core: unit tests; the Android app's tests read a file written by this core, and this core read a file written
  by the Android app's code; on seven real books (FB2, EPUB, FB3) places made by this app are found by the Android app.
- The settings window: built, tested and linted on both shells; not yet tried by hand on the Mac or on Linux.
- Linux: built and linted in a container; the GTK shell's sync on focus changes was not tried by hand, and nothing was
  measured on a Raspberry Pi 5 for this release. Syncing through Dropbox, iCloud Drive and Syncthing themselves was not
  tried.
